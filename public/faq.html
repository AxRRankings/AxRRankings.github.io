FAQ

The usual questions, answered.

Where do my tiers come from?

From your roles on the AxR Discord server, such as HT3 SWORD. When staff change your roles, your card updates by itself within a couple of minutes.

Why is the editor locked?

You need to be in the server and have at least one tier role. If you were just tested, wait a minute and press the check again button.

The 1card command says I have no card.

Open the editor and press Publish card. Only published cards can be posted. Saving a draft is not enough.

How many cards can I have?

Nine designs, one per slot. Only the slot you published last is shown in Discord.

Can I use my own images?

Yes. Add a background, banner or avatar by link or by uploading a file. Uploads are shrunk automatically to keep cards fast.

Can I show someone else's card?

Yes. Use 1card and mention the player. It works when they have published a card. The rankings page also lets you open anyone's card.

What do you store about me?

Your Discord ID, your username and your card designs. Nothing else. See the privacy page for details.

My rank changed but the rankings page did not.

The rankings refresh every few minutes. Check again shortly.
