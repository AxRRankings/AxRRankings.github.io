What's new on AxR Battle Cards.

New

Custom layouts and templates

November 2026

  • Layout editor. In the editor, open the new Layout tab and tap Open layout designer. Pick a starting point, then drag and resize every part of your card: avatar, name, rank, points, progress bar and tiers.
  • Share your layout. Publish it as a template so everyone can use it. Templates only carry the layout, so each player keeps their own colors, background and pictures.
  • Templates page. Browse layouts made by other players and apply one with a single tap.
  • 12 new fonts. Bungee, Bangers, Black Ops One, Bebas Neue, Anton, Monoton, Metal Mania, Creepster, Pirata One, Michroma, Rubik Glitch and Blackout Midnight.
  • Layout designer. Editing a layout now opens on its own full page, and you can Remix any template from the Templates page.
  • Post to Discord from the designer. Post your card to the server, or download it as a PNG, without leaving the layout designer.
  • New tier box styles. Pixel, Glow, Flat, Badge and Split looks for the small tier boxes, plus controls for corner roundness and box height (Tiers tab).
  • Updates page. You are here. New changes will show up at the top.
