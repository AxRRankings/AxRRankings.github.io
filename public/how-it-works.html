How it works

From tier test to a card in your channel, in four steps.

Get tier tested

Take a tier test in the AxR Discord server. Staff give you a tier role for each mode you were tested in, for example HT3 SWORD.

Log in with Discord

The site only asks for your Discord name and avatar. Your tiers are read from your roles on the server, so there is nothing to type in.

Design your card

Open the editor and change the layout, colors, font, background, avatar, border and which tiers show. Changes save automatically. You have nine slots for different designs.

Publish and post

Publish the slot you like best. Then type 1card in Discord to post it, or 1card @someone to show another player's card.

Ranked players only

You need at least one tier role and to be in the server. If you have no rank yet, the editor stays locked until you do.

Points and combat rank

Every tier is worth points. Add up your best tier in each mode and you get your combat rank.

Ready?
