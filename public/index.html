Questions

Where do my tiers come from?

Straight from your roles on the AxR server (like HT3 SWORD). When your rank changes, your card updates by itself within a couple of minutes.

Why can't I make a card?

You need to be in the server and hold at least one tier role. If you just got tested, wait a minute and refresh.

Can I show someone else's card?

Yes. Use /card and pick a member. It works if they have published a card.

Is my data safe?

We only store your Discord ID, your card design and its saved settings. We never get your password or messages.
