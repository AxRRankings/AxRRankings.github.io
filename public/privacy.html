Privacy & terms

What we collect

When you log in with Discord we receive your Discord user ID, username and avatar (the identify permission). We never see your password, messages or friends.

We store your Discord ID, your username, your card designs (colors, layout and any images you add) and which design you published.

What is public

  • Your server nickname, avatar, tiers and combat rank appear on the rankings page.
  • A card you publish can be viewed by anyone who has its link or uses the bot command.

Roles

The site reads your roles on the AxR server through the bot to show your tiers. It does not change any roles.

Removing your data

Ask server staff in the AxR Discord server and your saved designs will be deleted. You can also clear any slot yourself in the editor.

Terms

  • Do not upload images that are illegal, hateful or sexual. Staff can remove cards.
  • Cards are for the AxR community and may change or stop working at any time.
  • This project is not affiliated with Mojang Studios, Microsoft or Discord.
